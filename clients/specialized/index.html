Specialized Clients

Nostr isn't only for short posts. The same account works in apps for chat, video, music, writing, shopping and more.

Chat and groups​

0xchat​

Telegram-style private messages, group chats and calls.

  • Platforms: iPhone, Android, Desktop
  • Get it: 0xchat.com

White Noise​

A strongly encrypted messenger, like Signal, with no phone number needed. Still in beta.

  • Platforms: Android, iPhone
  • Get it: whitenoise.chat

Keychat​

Private chat with a bitcoin wallet built in.

  • Platforms: iPhone, Android, Desktop
  • Get it: keychat.io

Flotilla​

Discord-style community spaces with channels, chat and events.

  • Platforms: Web, iPhone, Android
  • Get it: flotilla.social

Flotilla treats a relay as a community "space". Inside a space you get rooms (channels) for chat, plus events. Because the group lives on a relay rather than inside one company's app, its members aren't locked into a single client.

Getting started with Flotilla:

  1. Visit flotilla.social or install the phone app.
  2. Log in with a browser extension, a signer or your key.
  3. Join a space someone has shared with you.
  4. Pick a room and start chatting.

Good for: open source projects, local meetups, topic communities and small teams.

Which apps keep messages most private?

Amethyst, 0xchat, Keychat, Coracle and Flotilla use the newer NIP-17 standard, which hides who is talking to whom. Damus is switching over in its next update. Primal still uses the older NIP-04 (as of October 2026), which leaks who messaged whom, and when. For anything sensitive, use a NIP-17 app or White Noise. See Direct messages.

Video and live​

  • Divine (iPhone, Android): six-second looping videos, the Vine reboot.
  • zap.stream (Web): live video streaming where viewers tip with zaps.
  • Nostr Nests and Corny Chat (Web): live audio rooms, like Twitter Spaces.

Music and podcasts​

  • Wavlake (Web, iPhone, Android): stream music, and artists get paid directly.
  • Fountain (iPhone, Android): a podcast app with Nostr comments, profiles and tips.

Writing and reading​

  • YakiHonne (Web, iPhone, Android): write and read long-form articles, plus a social feed and creator tools.
  • Pareto (Web): blogs and newsletters for independent journalists.
  • Wikifreedia (Web): a wiki where anyone can write their own version of an article.

Buy and sell​

  • Shopstr, Plebeian Market and Conduit (Web): marketplaces paid in bitcoin.

Get things done​

  • Formstr (Web): forms and surveys, like Google Forms. The same team makes Pages (shared docs) and Calendar.

Developers​

  • gitworkshop.dev (Web): Git repositories, issues and patches over Nostr.

Also useful​

  • Zapstore (Android): an app store full of Nostr apps, where developers sign every release.
  • Stacker News (Web): a Bitcoin forum that pays in sats. It isn't a Nostr app, but you can log in with Nostr and cross-post.

Handy tools​

  • nstart.me: guided account creation with a safe backup. Great for helping a friend join.
  • njump.me: opens any Nostr post or profile as a normal web page, so you can share it with people who aren't on Nostr.
  • nos.today and ants.sh: search across Nostr.
  • Mostr: a bridge to the fediverse (Mastodon and friends). There is no maintained bridge to Bluesky.

Media hosting​

Images and videos you post live on media servers. The standard for these is called Blossom, so your files aren't tied to one app. Most apps pick a server for you. Some let you add a backup server in Settings → Media, so your pictures survive if one shuts down.

  • nostr.build and nostrcheck.me: upload images and video.

Find more apps​

  • nostrapps.com: a much bigger directory of apps.
  • nostr.net: another list of Nostr projects.
