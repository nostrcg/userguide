Every link on this page was checked on 22 October 2026. We removed many links from the older version of this page because they had died, and several old Nostr web addresses now lead to spam or gambling sites. If an old tutorial sends you somewhere not listed here, be careful.

Find people and posts​

ToolWhat it's for
nos.todaySearch across Nostr

Keep your key safe​

DeviceSigner
AndroidAmber (also on Zapstore)
ComputerAlby, nos2x (Chrome), nos2x-fox (Firefox)
iPhoneAegis (beta via TestFlight; Android version on GitHub too)
Power usersnsecBunker, a self-hosted remote signer

For backups, use a password manager (Bitwarden, 1Password, Proton Pass or your phone's built-in one) plus a paper copy. See Backup Your Keys.

Wallets for zaps​

Zaps are optional. If you want them:

WalletTypeNotes
Alby Hub + Alby GoSelf-custodyThe reference wallet for connecting to Nostr apps
Primal walletSelf-custodyBuilt into the Primal phone apps (not the website). Has its own recovery phrase, separate from your nsec.
ZeusSelf-custodyMore control, a bit more to learn
CoinosCustodialFree and web-based. Disclosed a credential leak in August 2026, so pocket money only.
Minibits, Cashu.meEcash (custodial)The mint holds the funds. Pocket money only.

nwc.dev lists the wallets and apps that support Nostr Wallet Connect. More in Zaps & Lightning.

Media hosting​

Most apps upload pictures for you. If you need to upload directly, use nostr.build or nostrcheck.me. The standard for Nostr media servers is called Blossom.

ResourceWhat it's for

Most people never need to change relays. See Understanding Relays.

Other networks​

  • There is no maintained bridge to Bluesky.

Scams

Nostr has no token, so any "Nostr token" offer is a scam. Nostr also has no support desk: nobody legitimate will ever ask for your nsec.
